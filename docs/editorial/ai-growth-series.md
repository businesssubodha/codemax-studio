## Publication process

The ChatGPT scheduled task reads this repository and the calendar each morning, checks the next due article's original sources for material changes, and publishes at most one due item. It can use `scripts/publish-ai.mjs` to generate the two publication manifests after reviewing the article. It then builds, validates and commits through GitHub. Cloudflare's existing integrations deploy the result. Dates are target days rather than exact delivery times; a missed day is caught up one article at a time. No paid service was added.

A source that cannot be verified or an important unresolved factual change should hold that article for review and be reported, not silently published as current news. Record any later research check accurately. These are explanatory articles, not invented breaking-news reports or hands-on product reviews. Future drafts stay outside public archives, routing and the sitemap until released.

## Content and visual standards

Every article has a distinct purpose, original explanatory text, descriptive title, unique metadata, primary-source hyperlinks and internal AI archive links. Six original orange-and-charcoal SVG illustrations explain workflows, evidence review, conversations, hardware, learning and creative work. They are diagrams, not fabricated screenshots. Existing article templates add canonical URLs, BlogPosting structured data and publication dates. No unsupported ratings, client outcomes or ranking guarantees are included.
