## Choice of articles

- Free hosting: a complete operating-cost checklist, distinct from older hosting-provider reviews.
- Templates: a pre-purchase test with real content, distinct from existing ecommerce template lists.
- User testing: observed customer tasks, distinct from the queued contact-form accessibility checklist.
- Designer near me: a supplier shortlisting process, supporting the existing Melbourne service page rather than replacing it.
- WordPress: a content editing and publishing brief, distinct from maintenance and security articles.

Broad terms such as website and website design do not each receive a near-duplicate article. Branded login/support terms and development courses are not priorities for attracting CodeMax website clients. Existing builder and PageSpeed posts remain the starting points for those topics. Each new article has its own intent, descriptive title and service/enquiry links.

The new drafts extend the approved daily queue through 20 October. They remain unpublished until their scheduled release. No new paid services were added. Indexing and rankings must be evaluated using subsequent Search Console data, not inferred from a successful build.
